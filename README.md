## About

SONORA turns song recognition into a quick game. Start a room, share the code, and see who can name each track from its preview. The interface is available in English and Thai.

## Game modes

| Mode | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| **Classic** | Compete over timed rounds and earn points by identifying songs quickly. |
| **Song Bomb** | Name a song or artist before the timer runs out. Stay in the game with your remaining lives. |
| **Solo Play** | Test your music knowledge on your own. |

Choose from music genres and adjust round settings such as difficulty and answer choices. Song previews and artwork are looked up through the Apple iTunes catalog.
